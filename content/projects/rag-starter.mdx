## Overview

This starter focuses on clean abstractions for document loaders, chunking, embeddings, vector stores, and reranking. It is designed for fast iteration and strong retrieval quality.

### Highlights

- Pluggable vector DB and embedding providers
- Clean interfaces for evaluators and rerankers
- Built‑in tracing to surface latency and recall
